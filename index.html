How a Film Gets Made

From the first conversation to the final mix, every stage serves the story.

Phase 1: Story, Site & Research

We agree the audience, the change the film should make and the story that gets you there. We research the place, the people and any archive, then check access, airspace and permissions.

Phase 2: Production

Aerial and ground footage, interviews and location sound, planned around wildlife, visitors and protected sites.

Phase 3: Post & Delivery

Script, edit and grade. Add human narration, original music and sound design where commissioned, then deliver masters and social cuts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about story films, protected sites, narration, licensing and turnaround.

What kinds of films do you make?

Story-led short films for conservation, heritage, architecture and landscape projects. We can make campaign films, funder and supporter films, interpretation pieces, archive records and social cuts, always built around a human story.

Who do you work with?

Conservation and environment organisations, heritage and historic sites, architects and landscape practices, and owners of exceptional homes and estates. We also work with charities, community groups and other production teams.

Can you film on protected or sensitive sites?

Yes. We plan around protected landscapes, wildlife, landowners and public access, and we confirm permissions and airspace before the shoot. Our sub-250g aircraft let us work discreetly around buildings, visitors and sensitive habitats.

Do you film interviews, archive and location sound?

Yes. A story film can include interviews, archive material, location sound and ground-level footage alongside the aerial work. We can also work with your existing archive or footage.

Can you show change over time?

Yes. We program repeatable GPS waypoint missions, so each visit captures an identical flight path and framing. That gives you a clean like-for-like timeline for conservation, restoration or construction monitoring.

Do you offer narration, original music and sound design?

Yes. Narration, original music and sound design are available on any film. Every script is written by a human and every narration performance is by a professional human voice actor. Music is composed to picture, and sound design is mixed in-house at 24-bit/48kHz.

How long is a film, and where can it be used?

Most story films run from 60 to 180 seconds, with shorter social cuts where needed. We deliver horizontal masters and vertical versions, and licensing is included so you can publish worldwide without further clearance.

How long does it take?

Aerial records and inspections are typically delivered within 48 hours of the shoot. Story films take longer because of research, scripting, narration and post-production, usually two to four weeks depending on scope.

Where do you work?

We are based in Weston-super-Mare, North Somerset, and cover Bristol, Bath and the wider South West of England as our core service area. We also travel for the right project.

Are you CAA registered and insured?

Yes. All commercial operations are backed by third-party aviation liability insurance to EC785/2004, and we are registered with the Civil Aviation Authority as operator GBR-OP-WT9QWY4TY8KC and remote pilot GBR-RP-GVPVYRL8PV3T.

Do you use AI voiceovers or scripts?

No. We do not use AI-generated narration or AI-written scripts. Every script is written by a human, and every narration performance is by a professional human voice actor.
