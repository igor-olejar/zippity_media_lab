Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about drone filming, licensing, turnaround and areas covered.

Do I need a drone licence to have aerial footage filmed of my property?

No. All of our drone operations are flown by a registered remote pilot in the CAA A1 Open sub-category using sub-250g aircraft. You are hiring a compliant commercial operator, not operating the drone yourself, so the legal duty to be qualified sits with us. We will also confirm airspace and any permissions before flying.

Are you a drone company in Bristol?

We are based in Weston-super-Mare, North Somerset, and cover Bristol, Bath and the wider South West of England as our core service area. Bristol city centre is roughly 22 miles from our base, and we schedule Bristol shoots regularly.

What can you film with a sub-250g drone?

Sub-250g aircraft can legally fly closer to people and structures than heavier drones, which makes them well suited to estate agent marketing, exterior and garden footage, and tight construction site framing. We shoot 4K HDR at up to 60fps with 10-bit D-Log M capture for colour grading.

Can you film my construction site every month from the same angle?

Yes. We program repeatable GPS waypoint missions, so each visit captures an identical flight path and framing. That gives you a clean like-for-like progress timeline you can drop straight into client or stakeholder updates.

Can I get video, voiceover and music from the same supplier?

Yes. Aerial video, voiceover recording, custom scoring and final mastering are all handled in-house, so there is no third-party hand-off and no delay waiting on an external editor or studio. Master audio is delivered at 24-bit/48kHz WAV.

Who performs the voiceover, and can you write the script?

Voiceovers are performed by professional voice actors, and we can write the script from scratch or work from your own draft. We then record, mix and master it in-house alongside the custom score.

How fast can you turn footage around?

Standard turnaround is 48 hours from shoot to final master, including colour grading, multi-track audio mixing and voiceover sync. Rush delivery is available on request and subject to shoot volume.

Are you insured for commercial drone work?

Yes. All commercial operations are backed by third-party aviation liability insurance to EC785/2004, and we are registered with the Civil Aviation Authority as operator GBR-OP-WT9QWY4TY8KC and remote pilot GBR-RP-GVPVYRL8PV3T.

Can agencies and developers sub-contract you for white-label delivery?

Yes. We regularly work as a white-label production partner for agencies and developers. You get unbranded masters, full audio licensing included so you can publish worldwide without further clearance, and delivery within 48 hours.
